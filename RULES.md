# Platform Rules

- `SPECIFICATIONS.md` is the source of truth.
- Add a reusable package only when at least one approved product requirement needs it.
- Keep package interfaces small, documented, and independently testable.
- Do not copy product secrets, customer data, or private commercial information into this repository.
- Do not assign GPIOs, electrical ratings, enclosure dimensions, or safety limits without an approved product specification or hardware file.
- KiCad-generated output is a candidate design until human review and ERC/DRC evidence are complete.
- Use absolute local paths in operational documentation.
